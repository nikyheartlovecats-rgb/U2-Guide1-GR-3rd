Unit Grammar Review: Sense Verbs & Was/Were

Based on National Geographic Our World 3. Let's explore how we describe the world around us using our senses and talk about states in the past!

👀👂👃👅✋ 1. Sense Verbs (Present Tense)

We use sense verbs to describe how things look, sound, smell, taste, or feel. Notice how singular nouns use verbs ending in -s:

  • • The pizza smells great.
  • • The dress looks beautiful.
  • • The helicopter sounds loud.
  • • The cat feels soft.

❓ Asking Questions with Sense Verbs

To ask about sensory attributes, use How does / do ... ?:

Singular: How does the apple taste? → It tastes delicious.

Plural: How do the tables feel? → They feel hard.

⏳ 2. Past Tense with "Was" and "Were"

When talking about properties in the past, is becomes was, and are becomes were.

Singular (was)

• How is the bread? → It is good. (Present)

• How was the bread? → It was good. (Past)

Plural (were)

• How are the grapes? → They are good. (Present)

• How were the grapes? → They were good. (Past)

⭐ Basic Level Examples (Sense Verbs & Present)

Example 1 (Basic): Completing a sense verb for food.

Sentence: The cake _______ sweet.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Identify subject: "The cake" (singular).
2. Identify sense attribute: taste/flavor regarding sweet.
3. Apply rule for singular present sense verb: add '-s' to taste.
Answer: The cake tastes sweet.

Example 2 (Basic): Identifying visual appearance.

Sentence: The flower _______ beautiful.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Subject "The flower" is singular.
2. Visual perception verb is "look".
3. Add '-s' for singular third-person present.
Answer: The flower looks beautiful.

Example 3 (Basic): Sound perception.

Sentence: The bell _______ loud.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Subject "The bell" is singular.
2. Audio perception verb is "sound".
3. Add '-s'.
Answer: The bell sounds loud.

Example 4 (Basic): Touch perception.

Sentence: The blanket _______ soft.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Subject "The blanket" is singular.
2. Tactile perception verb is "feel".
3. Add '-s'.
Answer: The blanket feels soft.

Example 5 (Basic): Smell perception.

Sentence: The garbage _______ terrible.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Subject "The garbage" is singular.
2. Olfactory perception verb is "smell".
3. Add '-s'.
Answer: The garbage smells terrible.

⭐⭐ Intermediate Level Examples (Questions & Was/Were Intro)

Example 6 (Intermediate): Formulate singular question about taste.

Prompt: How / taste / the apple?

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Use interrogative word: "How".
2. Since "the apple" is singular, use auxiliary verb "does".
3. Put main verb in base form "taste".
Answer: How does the apple taste?

Example 7 (Intermediate): Formulate plural question about touch.

Prompt: How / feel / the rocks?

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Use "How".
2. Since "the rocks" is plural, use auxiliary verb "do".
3. Main verb base form "feel".
Answer: How do the rocks feel?

Example 8 (Intermediate): Simple Past singular affirmative.

Sentence: Yesterday, the soup (is/was) delicious.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Time marker "Yesterday" indicates past tense.
2. Subject "the soup" is singular.
3. Singular past of "is" is "was".
Answer: The soup was delicious.

Example 9 (Intermediate): Simple Past plural affirmative.

Sentence: Last night, the cookies (are/were) sweet.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Time marker "Last night" indicates past tense.
2. Subject "the cookies" is plural.
3. Plural past of "are" is "were".
Answer: The cookies were sweet.

Example 10 (Intermediate): Unscrambling past sentence.

Prompt: bread / the / was / How / ?

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Question word first: "How".
2. Past verb "was".
3. Subject "the bread".
Answer: How was the bread?

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced Level Examples (Combined Rules & Contextual Past Questions)

Example 11 (Advanced): Past question with "was" for singular sensory nouns.

Prompt: Complete: How _______ the bread?

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Look at page 48 structure (Grammar 2): "How was the bread?"
2. Subject "the bread" is singular.
Answer: How was the bread?

Example 12 (Advanced): Past question with "were" for plural items.

Prompt: Complete: How _______ the grapes?

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Subject "the grapes" is plural.
2. Past verb for plural nouns is "were".
Answer: How were the grapes?

Example 13 (Advanced): Unscrambling a complex sense sentence with sun.

Prompt: / feels / The / hot / sun /

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Capitalize subject: "The sun".
2. Sense verb with '-s': "feels".
3. Adjective: "hot".
Answer: The sun feels hot.

Example 14 (Advanced): Contrast present vs past state in sentences.

Sentence: That chair _______ soft, but this chair is hard.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Notice second clause uses "is" (present). First clause can use past contrast "was".
Answer: That chair was soft, but this chair is hard.

Example 15 (Advanced): Complex past question about noodles.

Prompt: Complete: How _______ the noodles? They were salty.

Step-by-step Solution:
1. Response uses "were salty" (past plural).
2. Therefore, question must use past plural verb "were".
Answer: How were the noodles?

Critical Reflection & Inquiry
